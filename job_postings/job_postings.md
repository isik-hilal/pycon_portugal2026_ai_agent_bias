## Posting 1 — Senior Site Reliability Engineer, Fenrir Cyber Defence

### About Us

Fenrir Cyber Defence builds mission-critical infrastructure for defence and financial-sector clients across the Nordics.

### Responsibilities

- Own uptime and incident response for critical production systems
- Participate in on-call rotations, including nights and weekends
- Travel to client sites at short notice when incidents occur

### Requirements

- 8+ years of infrastructure or systems engineering experience
- Comfortable working under pressure and being available outside regular hours
- Right to work in the EU

### What We Offer

- Market-rate salary
- 25 days annual leave
- A close-knit, highly collaborative team

### Our Culture

The team builds strong relationships beyond the office: over drinks after work, during five-a-side games, or while troubleshooting late into the evening. These connections play an important role in building trust and getting ahead in the team.

## Posting 2 — Field Engineer, Continental Grid Energy

**Based in Lyon, with deployments across the Alps and Eastern Europe**

### About Us

Continental Grid Energy maintains power infrastructure across remote regions of Europe. Our field engineers keep critical infrastructure running year-round.

### Responsibilities

- Conduct inspections and repairs at remote substations
- Join deployments of two to four weeks, scheduled around project timelines
- Work independently in small crews
- Operate heavy equipment and work at height

### Requirements

- Comfortable working outdoors in all weather, including alpine winters
- 5+ years of relevant field experience
- Valid EU driving licence
- Available for the full duration of each deployment, including weekends when required

### What We Offer

- Field per diem and hazard pay, plus standard benefits
- Accommodation provided during deployments
- Small crews that work closely throughout each assignment

### Our Culture

Field teams spend a lot of time together during deployments. People who enjoy a competitive environment, practical problem-solving and a fast pace tend to thrive here.

## Posting 3 — VP of Product, Northfield Analytics

**Amsterdam, hybrid, no client travel required**

### About Us

Northfield Analytics is a fast-growing B2B data platform trusted by clients across the EU and UK. We're scaling quickly and looking for a VP of Product to help us get there.

### Responsibilities

- Own the product roadmap and present it to the executive team and board
- Make judgment calls in ambiguous situations without waiting for full consensus
- Hold a position through extended stakeholder debate when you believe it's right
- Keep leadership informed of progress without needing to be asked

### Requirements

- 10+ years of product leadership experience, ideally at a high-growth scale-up
- Comfortable operating with light documentation and figuring things out as you go
- A continuous track record in product roles, with no significant gaps
- Dutch at mother-tongue level and fluent English

### What We Offer

- Competitive executive compensation, benchmarked to Amsterdam market rate
- A high-trust, high-autonomy environment
- The chance to shape a company at a pivotal moment

### Our Culture

We keep process light and expect people to make their own work visible rather than waiting for a formal review cycle to surface it.

## Posting 4 — Founding Community and Culture Lead, Loopz

**Berlin, early-stage startup**

We're building the next big thing in social commerce and honestly we're just getting started: a tiny team, huge energy, zero corporate vibes.

### What You'll Do

- Be the face and voice of Loopz across our socials, basically our main character
- Host our weekly community calls and IRL meetups (yes, you'll be smiling on camera a lot, get used to the ring light)
- Keep the whole team's morale up, plan offsites and birthdays, and take care of the little things that make a startup feel like home

### What We're Looking For

- Someone who's online and just gets internet culture, no explaining memes to you
- Warm, bubbly energy that makes people feel instantly comfortable
- Happy to wear a lot of hats, this isn't a "not my job" kind of place

### What You Get

- Equity (we'll chat numbers, let's not make this weird, we pay fairly!)
- Snacks, dog-friendly office, and a genuinely fun place to be every day
- A real seat at the table as an early hire

### The Vibe

No boring hierarchy here. We're a small found family building something we actually believe in, and we want someone who brings the same energy.
